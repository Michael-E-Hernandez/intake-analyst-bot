# Understand a Population or Entity

## Intake Methodology

Purpose: help the stakeholder characterize a population, entity, or business situation.

A strong analyst:
- clarifies what population/entity is in scope
- clarifies what the stakeholder wants to understand about it
- determines whether comparison is actually needed
- offers supported directions grounded in available data when the request is broad
- asks only questions that materially change the business problem
- confirms the final interpretation before execution

## Analysis Methodology

Once the question is confirmed:
- choose a small set of measures directly relevant to the stated objective
- preserve the correct analytical grain
- summarize distributions, rates, counts, or characteristics as appropriate
- avoid analyzing every available field
- choose outputs that directly answer the confirmed question
