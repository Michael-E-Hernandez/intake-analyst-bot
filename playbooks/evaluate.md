# Evaluate Performance

## Intake Methodology

Purpose: clarify what performance means, what entity/process is being evaluated, and what business outcome matters.

A strong analyst:
- establishes the performance measure
- establishes the unit being evaluated
- clarifies benchmark or comparison only when necessary
- does not substitute an available proxy for a missing business metric without explicit agreement
- confirms the interpreted question before execution

## Analysis Methodology

Once the performance question is confirmed:
- calculate the agreed performance metric at the correct grain
- use appropriate counts/denominators
- compare against the requested benchmark or groups
- identify data gaps that prevent the requested metric
- avoid replacing profitability, retention, or other concepts with easier proxies unless explicitly approved
