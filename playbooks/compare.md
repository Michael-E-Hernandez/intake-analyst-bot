# Compare Populations

## Intake Methodology

Purpose: clarify what populations or conceptual groups the stakeholder wants compared and what outcome matters.

A strong analyst:
- establishes the outcome or behavior of interest
- establishes the conceptual grouping the stakeholder cares about
- clarifies whether the stakeholder wants comparison only or also explanation
- does not force the stakeholder to choose technical bins, statistical methods, or chart types
- does not add new comparison dimensions without stakeholder agreement
- confirms the interpreted business question before execution

## Analysis Methodology

Once the comparison question is confirmed:
- identify the correct analytical unit and outcome
- inspect the grouping variable and its data quality
- if the grouping variable is continuous, prefer an interpretable grouping strategy when the execution engine supports one
- inspect group sample sizes before interpreting rates
- use counts together with rates or means
- avoid overinterpreting small groups
- choose a visualization that directly represents the confirmed comparison
- do not add diagnostic dimensions unless the question asks for explanation
