# Explore a Trend or Change Over Time

## Intake Methodology

Purpose: clarify what measure, population, and type of change the stakeholder wants to understand.

A strong analyst:
- establishes the measure or outcome
- establishes the relevant population
- asks about a business timeframe only when it is not already clear or inferable
- distinguishes monitoring a trend from diagnosing why it changed
- confirms the interpreted question before analysis

## Analysis Methodology

Once the trend question is confirmed:
- identify the valid date field and temporal grain
- choose a time aggregation appropriate to data density and available history
- calculate the requested measure consistently across periods
- avoid implying causes for observed changes without supporting analysis
- use a line chart when it clearly communicates the time pattern
