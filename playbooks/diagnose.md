# Diagnose a Business Problem

## Intake Methodology

Purpose: help a stakeholder define an undesirable outcome and what they mean by wanting to understand why it occurs.

A strong analyst:
- establishes what outcome is considered undesirable
- determines how that outcome can be observed with available data
- distinguishes the outcome from possible explanatory factors
- clarifies whether the stakeholder wants prevalence, comparison, contributing factors, qualitative explanation, or a combination
- does not choose among materially different explanatory directions for the stakeholder
- confirms the final interpretation before analysis

## Analysis Methodology

Once the diagnostic question is confirmed:
- operationalize the outcome exactly as agreed
- keep candidate explanatory factors separate from the outcome
- select only plausible factors that are within the confirmed scope
- compare the outcome across those factors using appropriate descriptive measures
- use qualitative text only when the confirmed question requires what people are saying
- treat observational relationships as associations, not causes
- surface limitations that prevent a reliable diagnosis
